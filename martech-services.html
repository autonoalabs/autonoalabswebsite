A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

From a Website Enquiry to Sales Follow-up

A customer completes your website form. The CRM keeps the contact and enquiry together, the right team receives a follow-up task and the report records what happened next.

  1. Capture the enquiryKeep the contact details, enquiry source and preferences together.
  2. Follow up with ownershipAssign the task and connect the next agreed action or campaign step.
