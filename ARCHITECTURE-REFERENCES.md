# Architecture and logo references

These 24 examples are curated starting points, rather than every possible technology combination. The diagrams show logical system flows, not production network topology. Each includes a point to review for reliability, access, cost, integration or recovery.

For India and Gulf projects, confirm customer-specific data and backup locations, regional service/model availability, latency, support and applicable requirements. No technology stack alone guarantees compliance.

## Logo provenance

Local SVGs preserve the original artwork and use a small neutral plate for contrast against the dark website theme. The exact source for each is recorded in assets/tech-logos/manifest.json.

Most logos come from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon) (MIT project licence) and [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0 project licence). The dbt SVG comes from the [dbt documentation repository](https://github.com/dbt-labs/docs.getdbt.com/blob/current/website/static/img/dbt-logo.svg). AWS uses the logo in Devicon v2.17.0. Product logos remain subject to vendor trademark rights and guidelines. Showing products in example architectures does not imply an Autonoa Labs partnership or certification.
