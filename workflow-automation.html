A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

A Supplier Invoice Approval

An invoice arrives, the accounts team checks it and the right person approves it. The workflow then updates the accounts system and records the result. Payment approval stays with the responsible team.

  1. Receive and checkKeep the invoice and supplier record together. Highlight missing information.
  2. Ask for approvalSend the task to the right person and wait for their decision.
  3. Update and confirmApply the approved update, record the result and flag failures.

This is an example of a possible setup. We review your existing tools and requirements before agreeing a design.

What We Check Before Building

  • Which task takes the most time today, and who is responsible for each step?
  • Which systems need to exchange information, and how can they connect?
  • Which changes need approval, and how should failed or incorrect steps be handled?
