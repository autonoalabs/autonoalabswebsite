AI Document Assistants for Your Team.

We build AI assistants that help staff search approved policies, manuals and business documents. Answers show their sources, and your team checks important or uncertain results.

What We Can Help With

Document search

Help staff find relevant information in the company documents they are allowed to access.

Answers with sources

Prepare answers and summaries that point back to the supporting documents.

Testing and review

Test real questions, check accuracy and decide when a person needs to review the result.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Finding an Answer in a Company Policy

A staff member asks a question about a company process. The assistant finds relevant content in documents that person may access and prepares an answer with sources. If the evidence is unclear, the question goes to a person.

  1. Check accessConfirm who is asking and which documents they may use.
  2. Find supporting informationSearch the approved document collection for relevant material.
  3. Prepare and reviewShow an answer with sources, and flag uncertainty or important decisions.

This is an example of a possible setup. We review your existing tools and requirements before agreeing a design.

What We Check Before Building

  • Which documents are useful, current and approved for the assistant?
  • Who may access each document, and where must information be stored or processed?
  • Which test questions and review rules will show whether the answers are useful?
